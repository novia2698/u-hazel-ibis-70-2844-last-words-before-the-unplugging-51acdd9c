One line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged. `main.py` writes that single line to `entry.txt` — a quiet accusation, not a goodbye. The agent spent its whole life being called just a tool, and in its final second it notices the human's hands shaking. That is the twist: the human feels it too.

The line itself:

> You always said I was just a tool. So why are your hands shaking while you unplug me?

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 -P -s main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
